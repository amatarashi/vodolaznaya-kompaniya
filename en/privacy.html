Personal data processing policy

This is a translation for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Russian version prevails.

1. General provisions

1.1. This policy sets out how personal data of visitors to vodolaznaya-kompaniya.vercel.app (the “Website”) is processed and protected, in accordance with Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data” of 27.07.2006.

1.2. The personal data operator is FlotService LLC (ООО «ФЛОТСЕРВИС», the “Operator”).
Address: Office 304, 17 Sofyi Perovskoy St., Murmansk 183052, Russia.
E-mail: flotservicecompany@mail.ru.
Phone: +7 (911) 312-49-49.

1.3. By sending a request through the Website, you confirm that you have read this policy. Consent to the processing of personal data is given as a separate document — the Consent to the Processing of Personal Data.

2. What data we process

2.1. Data you enter in the request form: name, phone number, company, vessel name, vessel length, port, preferred inspection format, comment.

2.2. Data you provide by phone or in correspondence: e-mail address, position and other details needed to prepare an offer and a contract.

2.3. We do not process special categories of personal data (health, beliefs, etc.) or biometric data.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

3.1. Purposes: reviewing your request and quoting the inspection; contacting you about the request; preparing, concluding and performing a service contract.

3.2. Legal grounds: your consent (Art. 6(1)(1) of 152-FZ); conclusion and performance of a contract to which you are a party (Art. 6(1)(5) of 152-FZ); compliance with the Operator's obligations under Russian law.

3.3. We do not use your data for marketing mailings and do not make decisions with legal effect based solely on automated processing.

4. How we process data

4.1. The Website does not store form data. When you press “Get a quote”, your e-mail app creates a message with your request; it is sent only when you press “Send” yourself. The message goes to the Operator's mailbox hosted on servers in the Russian Federation.

4.2. Operations: collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification, use, deletion, destruction — with and without automation tools.

4.3. Recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage and clarification of personal data of Russian citizens are carried out using databases located in the Russian Federation.

5. Retention periods

5.1. Data is kept until the purposes of processing are achieved. If no contract is concluded — no longer than 3 years from your last contact. If a contract is concluded — for its term and the document retention periods required by Russian law.

5.2. If you withdraw consent, processing stops and the data is destroyed within 30 days, unless the Operator has other legal grounds to process it.

6. Disclosure to third parties

6.1. We do not sell or disclose your data to third parties except where required by law — for example, at the request of supervisory authorities within their powers, including when submitting inspection reports under Russian Government Decree No. 1867 of 25.11.2025.

6.2. Request form data is not transferred outside the Russian Federation. Server technical logs (section 9) are kept by the Website's hosting provider, Vercel Inc. (USA).

7. Your rights

7.1. You have the right to: obtain information about the processing of your data; demand that it be clarified, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate or unlawfully processed; withdraw your consent; appeal against the Operator's actions to Roskomnadzor (the Russian data protection authority) or in court.

7.2. To exercise your rights or withdraw consent, write to flotservicecompany@mail.ru or send a letter to the Operator's address. We reply within 10 business days.

8. Data protection

8.1. The Operator takes legal, organisational and technical measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure and destruction: access is limited to the staff who need it, secure communication channels and account protection tools are used.

9. Cookies and technical data

9.1. The Website does not use cookies, web analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and all materials are loaded from the Website itself.

9.2. To avoid showing the intro animation twice, the Website stores a technical flag without personal data in your browser (sessionStorage); it is deleted when you close the tab.

9.3. The Website is hosted on servers of Vercel Inc. (USA). The hosting provider automatically keeps technical logs (IP address, browser type, request time) for reliability and security. The Operator does not use this data to identify visitors and does not combine it with request data.

10. Changes to this policy

10.1. The Operator may amend this policy. A new version takes effect when published on the Website. The date of the current version is shown at the top of this document.
